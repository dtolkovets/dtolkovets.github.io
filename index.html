— About

Mechanical engineer by training. Robot builder by choice.

I'm completing a BSc in Mechanical Engineering – Product Engineering and Context (MPEC) at TH Köln, expected August 2027. The programme is problem-based: projects end in a working system, an empirical evaluation, and a scientific paper.

We worked in teams of three to six, using agile methods and regularly reflecting on how we worked together.

Whenever possible, I steered towards robotics and AI. I chose Social Robotics as my specialisation, did my internship at Furhat Robotics, and the projects I worked on along the way took the same direction.
